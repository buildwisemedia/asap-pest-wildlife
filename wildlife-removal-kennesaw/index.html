Imagine a roofer finds a loose vent while preparing a repair. Keep a photo and ask whether there are signs of use. If animals may be inside, have that checked before the vent is sealed. Share the agreed work order with both teams so a finished repair does not hide an unresolved entry problem.

Pest control

What to note before you call

Ants

Tell the team about recent window, door, or kitchen work. Photos of the trail help connect the report with the area being changed.

Roaches

Mention open walls, plumbing work, and leaks in the repair zone. Those details help decide what can be checked before the space is closed.

Termites

Bring any past inspection or treatment report to a wood-repair visit. The cause of damage and the repair plan should be clear before boards are replaced.

Mosquitoes

If yard work changes where water collects, mark the spots that stay wet. Include covers and stored building materials that hold rain.

Spiders

Note the rooms or exterior spaces affected during building work. Keep the inspection area accessible without reaching into hidden gaps yourself.

Fleas and ticks

Explain any room closures, yard projects, and pets at the home. Work dates and access limits can affect a suitable treatment schedule.

Pest and Wildlife Removal in Kennesaw, Georgia FAQ

Common questions

Does every wildlife repair in Kennesaw need a permit?

Do not assume either way. The city asks owners to contact Building Services when they are unsure. Describe the exact repair and confirm whether the address is within city limits.

Should the roofer seal an opening right away?

If there are signs of active wildlife use, arrange a check first. The animal and any young inside can affect when an opening should be closed.

Can the inspection use photos from my contractor?

Yes, share them along with the repair notes and work date. They give useful context, but they do not replace a check of current conditions.

What should a repair proposal explain?

Ask which openings are included, what materials will be used, how removal is timed, and who handles cleanup. Any warranty should describe the actual work and its terms.

How can I line up the visit with planned work?

Call 770-691-3636 with your Kennesaw address and the contractor’s schedule. ASAP will confirm availability. Do not assume a same-day visit until the team agrees to it.
