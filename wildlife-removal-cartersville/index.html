Pest control

What to note before you call

Ants

Track whether the problem returns after meals, waste handling, or a delivery. A simple log can show which areas need a closer look.

Roaches

Include break areas, kitchens, and stored boxes in your notes. Tell us who can provide access when those rooms are in use.

Termites

If stored wood or a building part looks damaged, show both its location and condition. Keep treatment and structural repair questions separate.

Mosquitoes

Look for rain held in unused containers near storage areas. Report larger drainage concerns to the person responsible for that land.

Spiders

Describe quiet corners, seldom-used equipment, and webs near doors. The inspection should account for who works in or visits those spaces.

Fleas and ticks

Tell the team where animals rest or enter and whether the space serves both home and work use. That context helps plan safe access during treatment.

Pest and Wildlife Removal in Cartersville, Georgia FAQ

Common questions

What should I change around my Cartersville garbage cart?

Follow the city’s current curbside guide: bag and tie household trash, keep it inside, and let the lid close fully.

Can a garage or workshop be checked with the house?

Tell ASAP about each structure when you call. The team can confirm access and the requested scope. A house visit does not automatically include every outbuilding.

Why keep a record of repeat pest sightings?

Dates, rooms, deliveries, and open-door times can give an inspection useful context. A log also gives you a way to discuss whether activity changes after the agreed work.

Does a business need the same plan as a home?

The plan should account for the space, staff, stored goods, access times, and service needed. Explain those details before agreeing to treatment or a repeat visit schedule.

What should I have ready when I call?

Give your address, the areas involved, and the name of the access contact. Call 770-691-3636 so ASAP can confirm which services and visit times are available.
