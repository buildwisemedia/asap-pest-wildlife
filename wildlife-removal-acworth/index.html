At your Acworth property

Check the land as well as the animal

Acworth’s city wildlife guidance describes natural areas around Lake Acworth and Lake Allatoona, including U.S. Army Corps of Engineers land. The city notes that outdoor wildlife encounters can be part of living near these places.

City of Acworth wildlife guidance

For a concern by a lake or creek, say whether it is on your lot, common land, or public land. An animal sighting is different from damage to a building or a blocked water route. Do not enter the water, disturb a dam, or start work on land you do not control.

Bring a location, not just a lake name

If you find chewed wood near water, take a photo from stable ground. Mark the spot on a property map and note any change in water level. The team needs to identify the animal, land owner, and kind of damage before discussing a plan. A lake-area mailing address alone does not settle those points.

Bats

Pest control

What to note before you call

Ants

Tell us if the trail is on a patio, near stored food, or inside. Keep photos clear enough to show the insect as well as its route.

Roaches

Include sheltered outdoor storage and any indoor damp spots in the inspection notes. Location helps guide the next questions.

Termites

Show the team wood damage near steps, a deck, or the home itself. Wet or damaged wood should not be labeled termite damage without a check.

Mosquitoes

Distinguish small items holding water on your lot from the lake or a public drainage area. Each place has a different owner and scope of work.

Spiders

Record recurring webs on covered porches or stored gear. Tell the team what you use the space for before treatment is considered.

Fleas and ticks

Mention shoreline walks, yard use, and pets when describing the issue. Any treatment plan should account for those uses and the site.

Pest and Wildlife Removal in Acworth, Georgia FAQ

Common questions

Can ASAP remove wildlife from public land near Acworth?

Public or shared land needs the correct owner or authority involved. Confirm the location first. A request from a nearby homeowner does not grant permission to work there.

Should I remove a beaver dam myself?

Do not start by pulling it apart. Have the animal, water route, ownership, and required permissions checked. A removal plan and a drainage plan may involve different work.

Are all animals near Lake Acworth a problem?

No. The city describes wildlife encounters as part of the local natural setting. Focus on the actual damage or building access, and avoid feeding or approaching wildlife.

My mailing address says Acworth. Is that enough to confirm service?

Give ASAP the full street address and the kind of issue. Mailing names, city limits, and service availability are different things. The team will confirm the address and visit options.

What photos help with a water-edge concern?

Use views taken from a safe, dry place. Show the damage, nearby landmarks, and its position on your lot if known. Do not wade in or move branches for a better photo.
