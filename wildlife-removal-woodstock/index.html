Pest control

What to note before you call

Ants

Compare where trails appear in your kitchen and near common walkways. Report each place without applying products in a neighbor’s space.

Roaches

Let the team know if several units have reports. Access, food storage, and moisture checks may need one agreed schedule.

Termites

Ask who holds any existing termite records for the building. Bring the report or agreement so the new inspection has that context.

Mosquitoes

List shared planters, drains, and other places that hold water. The person who maintains them should be part of the next step.

Spiders

Report webs around common lights or entry doors to the building manager. A photo helps distinguish an active issue from an old web.

Fleas and ticks

Tell us which indoor rooms and shared pet areas are involved. Any common-area work needs clear notice and access arrangements.

Pest and Wildlife Removal in Woodstock, Georgia FAQ

Common questions

I rent in Woodstock. What should I tell my property manager?

Send the date, room or unit, photos, and a short description. Ask who can approve an inspection and repairs. Your lease and property arrangements determine the next step.

Can you inspect a shared attic without access to every unit?

The team will explain what can be checked with the access available. Missing access may limit the findings. Arrange keys or an approved contact before the visit.

Should neighbors book separate treatments for the same building?

Start by comparing the locations and dates of the reports. If there may be a shared route, ask for a plan that explains how inspections, work, and follow-up will fit together.

Does trail access make wildlife removal necessary?

No. A nearby trail is not evidence of animal entry. Focus on damage, repeated building activity, or signs indoors before choosing removal work.

What does ASAP need to schedule a Woodstock visit?

Provide the street address, unit number if any, the access contact, and the concern. Call 770-691-3636 to confirm availability and the work that can be inspected.
